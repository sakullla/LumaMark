# Chrome Web Store listing (English, United States)

## Name

LumaMark

## Summary

A local-first bookmark manager to save, organize, and sync bookmarks through your own WebDAV service.

## Category

Productivity

## Detailed description

LumaMark helps you save, organize, and synchronize browser bookmarks while keeping you in control of your data.

SAVE AND ORGANIZE
• Save the current page from the toolbar, the right-click menu, or Alt+Shift+L.
• Add tags, notes, and folders, then search by title, URL, tag, or note.
• Organize with list and grid views, filters, bulk editing, duplicate cleanup, and Trash.
• Undo changes and import or export browser HTML and LumaMark JSON.

SYNC AND PRIVACY
• Stay in two-way sync with Chrome's built-in bookmarks instead of maintaining a disconnected bookmark list.
• Keep data on this browser by default, with no LumaMark account required.
• Sync only after you configure your own WebDAV service.
• Your search address is not added to the sync file. LumaMark does not archive page bodies, sell data, or include ads or analytics tracking.

MORE FEATURES
• Does not replace your New Tab page.
• View, search, and organize local bookmarks while offline.
• Choose light, dark, or system theme.

LumaMark is for people who want a cleaner way to manage bookmarks and prefer synchronizing them through a WebDAV service they choose.
