# LumaMark Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

## English

LumaMark is a local-first bookmark manager. The developer does not operate a LumaMark account service, analytics service, advertising service, or bookmark-sync server.

### Information LumaMark handles

LumaMark stores bookmark URLs, titles, favicon addresses, tags, notes, folders, favorite status, ordering, revision information, and deleted-bookmark records in the browser's local extension storage. It also stores your interface preferences and sync settings locally.

When you save a page or link, LumaMark processes its URL and title and, when available, its favicon address. LumaMark does not archive webpage bodies, screenshots, PDF files, or permanent page snapshots.

If you configure WebDAV, LumaMark stores the WebDAV address, remote path, username, and password locally. The password is not shown again in the settings page. Your WebDAV username may contain personal information if you choose to use it.

### How information is used

LumaMark uses this information only to provide bookmark saving, organization, import/export, browser-bookmark integration, and synchronization features.

Without a configured WebDAV address, bookmarks remain in local extension storage and the browser's own bookmarks. When you configure and enable WebDAV sync, LumaMark transmits bookmark and folder records to the WebDAV server you specify. WebDAV credentials are sent to that server to authenticate the connection. No bookmark data or WebDAV credentials are sent to the developer.

Your search-engine address is stored only on the current browser and is not included in the WebDAV sync file. On Chrome, favicons can be read from the browser's local favicon cache.

### Sharing, advertising, and human review

LumaMark does not sell or rent user data, use it for advertising or user profiling, or share it with developer-selected third parties. The only external service that receives sync data is the WebDAV provider you choose and configure. That provider's own privacy policy and retention practices apply.

The developer does not routinely read user data. If you voluntarily include bookmarks, credentials, logs, or other personal information in a support request, that information is used only to respond to your request. Never include passwords in public support issues.

### Retention and deletion

Local bookmarks, deleted-bookmark records, and settings remain until you delete them, clear the extension's data, or remove the extension, subject to your browser's behavior. Deleted-bookmark records may remain in local storage and the sync manifest to propagate deletions.

Removing the extension does not delete the WebDAV file. You can delete that file through your WebDAV provider. You can export a LumaMark JSON backup before removing local data.

### Security

Use an HTTPS WebDAV endpoint to protect bookmarks and credentials in transit. LumaMark relies on the browser's extension storage and your WebDAV provider for the security of stored data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

### Limited use

LumaMark uses and transfers user data only to provide its user-facing bookmark-management features. It does not use or transfer user data for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising, and it does not allow developer personnel to read user data except for information a user explicitly provides for support or a legally required purpose.

### Contact

Questions and support requests can be submitted at:
https://github.com/sakullla/LumaMark/issues

# LumaMark 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

LumaMark 是本地优先的书签管理器。开发者不运营 LumaMark 账号服务、分析服务、广告服务或书签同步服务器。

## 处理的信息

LumaMark 在浏览器本地扩展存储中保存书签网址、标题、网站图标地址、标签、备注、文件夹、收藏状态、排序、变更版本和已删除书签记录，同时在本地保存界面偏好和同步设置。

保存页面或链接时，LumaMark 会处理其网址和标题，并在可用时处理图标地址。LumaMark 不保存网页正文、网页截图、PDF 文件或永久页面快照。

配置 WebDAV 后，LumaMark 会在本地保存 WebDAV 地址、远端路径、用户名和密码；密码不会再次显示在设置页中。如果你选择在用户名中使用个人信息，该用户名也会作为配置的一部分保存。

## 信息用途

这些信息仅用于提供书签保存、整理、导入导出、浏览器书签集成和同步功能。

未配置 WebDAV 时，书签仅保存在本地扩展存储和浏览器自带书签中。配置并启用 WebDAV 同步后，LumaMark 会把书签和文件夹记录传输到你指定的 WebDAV 服务器，并向该服务器发送凭据以完成认证。书签数据和 WebDAV 凭据不会发送给开发者。

搜索地址只保存在当前浏览器，不写入 WebDAV 同步文件。在 Chrome 上，网站图标可以从浏览器本地图标缓存读取。

## 共享、广告与人工读取

LumaMark 不出售或出租用户数据，不将其用于广告或用户画像，也不与开发者选择的第三方共享。唯一接收同步数据的外部服务是你自行选择和配置的 WebDAV 服务商；该服务商的隐私政策和数据保留规则同时适用。

开发者不会日常读取用户数据。如果你主动在支持请求中提供书签、凭据、日志或其他个人信息，这些信息仅用于响应该请求。请勿在公开支持议题中提交密码。

## 保留与删除

本地书签、已删除书签记录和设置会保留到你删除它们、清除扩展数据或移除扩展为止，具体取决于浏览器行为。为同步删除操作，已删除书签记录可能继续保留在本地存储和同步文件中。

移除扩展不会删除 WebDAV 文件；你可以通过 WebDAV 服务商删除该文件。清除本地数据前，可以先导出 LumaMark JSON 备份。

## 安全

请使用 HTTPS WebDAV 地址保护传输中的书签和凭据。LumaMark 依赖浏览器扩展存储和你的 WebDAV 服务商保护存储数据。没有任何存储或传输方式能够保证绝对安全。

## 有限使用

LumaMark 仅为提供面向用户的书签管理功能而使用和传输用户数据，不将其用于个性化、再营销或基于兴趣的广告。除用户明确为支持请求提供的信息或法律要求外，开发者人员不会读取用户数据。

## 联系方式

问题和支持请求请提交至：
https://github.com/sakullla/LumaMark/issues
